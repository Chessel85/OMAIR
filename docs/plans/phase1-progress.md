# Phase 1 progress log

Last updated 9 October 2026. This file records what has been done against `phase1.md`, so that work can resume in a new session. Update it at the end of each working session. Repository facts, the corpus location and shell notes are in `phase0-progress.md`.

## Stage status

- **Stage 1.0, decisions and open risks: done except the CI job (9 October 2026).** Its done condition is met: the spec decision is recorded, the survey has commercial engraver files, and the font test no longer warns on the known text fonts. The CI job waits for the real recogniser.
  - **Spec decision 11: done.** A clef, key or time signature equal to the one already in force is ignored in both files before the structure is compared (`docs/notes/evaluation-spec.md`, "Structural correctness" and decision 11, accepted by the owner at the Phase 0 sign-off). Code: `restated` and `signature_changes` in `src/omr/evaluate/events.py`, used by `_structure` in `metrics.py`. The damaged test recogniser now removes only a real change, and counts a second mismatch when the removal makes a later change a restatement. 4 new tests. `check_regression.py` and `check_harness.py --set regression` pass, and the regression baseline did not change.
  - **Baselines rescored** from the saved outputs into `evaluations/*-rule11` on the corpus drive. Note accuracy does not change. Structurally correct: Audiveris 9.0 to 22.5 percent, homr 11.3 to 17.7, LEGATO 25.0 to 32.1. On the real-world pairs: Audiveris 30.0 to 35.0, homr 22.5 to 30.0. The mismatches that remain are real ones (clefs, keys, bar counts, endings). Written up in `docs/notes/baselines.md`.
  - **Glyph-shape font test: done.** A font that is neither SMuFL nor on the legacy list, and has at least 10 glyphs on staves, is drawn glyph by glyph from its embedded font program. Each glyph coded as an ASCII letter or digit is compared with that character in the standard PDF fonts. If at least half of the font's glyph uses are drawn as their letters, the font is text and gets no warning. The rule and its calibration are in `docs/notes/inspector-spec.md`, "Letter-shape test". Code: `letter_share` in `src/omr/pdf/evidence.py`. 2 new tests. The 3 position tests now use a stand-in legacy font that draws "q" as a quarter note. Checked on the 387 survey pages with a suspect font: Edwin-Italic, FreeSerif, FreeSerifItalic, Liberation Serif and its italic are text, with no font warnings, and every music font keeps its class. It takes about 0.05 seconds a page, and only on pages where a font needs the test.
  - **Commercial engraver PDFs: done.** 21 IMSLP files (9 Sibelius, 6 Finale, 6 Dorico by their own metadata), chosen from IMSLP's edition notes, approved by the owner and downloaded by hand (IMSLP blocks scripted downloads with a bot check). They are in `sources/other/imslp` on the corpus drive, with licences in `sample.json`, and in the survey. Findings, in `docs/notes/pdf-survey.md`, "Commercial engraver sample": 16 files Type A, 4 Type B (outlined, all printed to PDF rather than exported), 1 a scan. Legacy fonts Opus, Helsinki and Maestro; Petrucci and November did not occur; Dorico uses SMuFL. Printer drivers rename the fonts, so the name list cannot find them. The legacy-font threshold (a quarter of glyphs on staves) holds: no main music font fell below 30 percent on 348 pages. No Type 3 font occurred, so that test is not done. Stage 1.1 in `phase1.md` is updated to match.
  - **Inspector faults found by the sample and fixed, with tests:** a Windows symbol font (Opus, codes U+F020 to U+F0FF) was taken for SMuFL; a beam drawn on a staff line made the line too thick and hid the staff; a landscape page stored sideways had no staves; and a stack of ledger lines could pass as a staff (now a staff must be at least 6 staff spaces long). The beam fix also finds staves the old code missed in MuseScore output: on a random sample of changed pages the new staff count matched the clef count on 114 of 128, against 1 for the old count.
  - **CI job for the real recogniser:** waits until the recogniser exists (Stage 1.3 or later).
- **Stage 1.1, glyph extraction and font mapping: done (9 October 2026).** Its done condition is met: every symbol class in the corpus is mapped, and `omr symbols` reports unmapped glyphs per file. The rules and results are in `docs/notes/symbol-spec.md`.
  - **Code:** `src/omr/pdf/symbols.py` (reading a page into named symbols and text runs), `smufl.py` (SMuFL names and reference outlines from the fonts Verovio installs, plus a supplement of about 60 names), `fonttables.py` (the Emmentaler table by glyph name; the Sonata table by byte for Opus, Helsinki and Maestro, and the companion table for OpusSpecial and HelsinkiSpecial), `shapes.py` (drawing glyphs and outlines and comparing them). Command `omr symbols FILE...` (`src/omr/symbol_report.py`), plain text and `--json`.
  - **Renamed fonts** (printer drivers' `TTE...t00`, `TT...t00`) are identified by drawing each glyph used and comparing it with the Bravura, Leland, Petaluma, Gootville and Leipzig reference for the symbol each table gives its byte. All 9 renamed fonts in the commercial sample are identified.
  - **Outlined symbols (Type B)** are named by shape. Checked by `scripts/check_outlines.py`: on the 43 Verovio PDFs of the regression set (outlined by the SVG route, checked against Verovio's own glyph list), 99.3 percent of glyphs found and 98.6 percent of named symbols right, the same without Leipzig, the font Verovio draws in, among the references. On 11 commercial files in Opus, Helsinki, Maestro and renamed fonts turned into outlines (`--type-a`), 98.5 percent found and 98.7 percent right.
  - **Corpus survey** (`scripts/run_survey.py symbols` and `symbols-report`, `survey/symbols-counts.txt`): 9.24 million symbols in 3,410 files. 26 font glyphs are unmapped (22 of them Leland's own optional glyph U+F631, which has no standard name; the other two Bravura glyphs are now named). 2,459 outlined shapes are not matched, 1,884 of them in one Type B file (bracket ends, outlined text, odd slurs). 51 files have anything unmapped.
  - **Fixes made along the way:** the shared log helper no longer stops on a character the console cannot show (a flat sign in a file name). fontTools is now a named dependency (`requirements.in`, CI).
  - **Tests:** `tests/test_symbols.py` (39), and CLI tests for `omr symbols`.
- **Stage 1.2, staves, systems and parts: done (9 October 2026).** Its done condition is met: on the regression set, 82 of 86 exact pairs (MuseScore 4) are right in parts, bars, clefs, keys and times (95.3 percent), and the 4 that are not have a barline the page cannot show (an invisible barline, and a barline in the middle of a bar). The rules, results and known limits are in `docs/notes/layout-spec.md`.
  - **Code:** `src/omr/pdf/layout.py` (staff lines including stacked hairlines, staves, systems joined by lines, braces or aligned barlines, barline groups with repeat dots, bars, attaching every symbol to a staff and step) and `src/omr/pdf/structure.py` (parts from braces and instrument names, systems with hidden staves matched by names and clefs, clefs including LilyPond's octave clefs, key signatures checked against the key order for the clef, time signatures, repeats, endings, navigation). The result is an `omr.evaluate.events.Score`, so decision 11 applies unchanged. Command `omr layout FILE...` (`src/omr/layout_report.py`) gives the structure in words.
  - **Check:** `scripts/check_structure.py` compares the structure read from each PDF with the ground truth, pair by pair. Regression set: exact pairs 76 of 86 structurally right (88.4 percent), 82 of 86 in the core items; engraver-input pairs 131 of 170 (77.1 percent), 148 of 170 in the core items. The 10 exact pairs not right are all known limits: an invisible barline, a mid-bar barline, an unprinted repeat count ("3 times"), and "Fine", "D.C. al Fine" or a segno stored in the ground truth as plain words without a playback marker.
  - **Development set** (1,778 pairs; a few rules were added from its MuseScore 4 failures: endings carried over to the next system, a key change to C at a system start, the fingering guard for endings): exact pairs 512 of 600 structurally right (85.3 percent), 560 of 600 in the core items (93.3 percent); engraver-input pairs 807 of 1,178 (68.5 percent), 895 of 1,178 in the core items (76.0 percent). Report in `evaluations/structure-development.txt` on the corpus drive. Verovio (bars, clefs, keys) and LilyPond (bars) are the weak engravers there; MuseScore's remaining failures include navigation words stored as plain text, one-line percussion staves, hidden staves in large scores, and mid-bar barlines.
  - **Tests:** `tests/test_layout.py` (19, including six regression pairs end to end), and a CLI test for `omr layout`. All 273 tests pass; `check_regression.py` passes unchanged.
