- **Stage 1.3, notation assembly: done (9 October 2026).** Its done condition is met: note accuracy is 96.7 percent over the 1,778 development pairs (at least 95 percent asked), with tuplet and onset errors reported separately. The rules, results and known limits are in `docs/notes/notation-spec.md`.
  - **Code:** `src/omr/pdf/notation.py`, called by `omr.pdf.structure.read_pdf(path, notes=True)`. Stems (vertical lines touching a notehead at the right edge going up or the left edge going down), beams (filled four-sided shapes, at least one end at a stem, so tremolo strokes are left out), flags, dots, rests, tuplet numbers (glyphs or italic text) over brackets or beam groups, grace and cue notes, ties (accidentals carried over barlines and system breaks), octave marks with their dashed lines, arpeggios, and the staff of a far note found from its ledger lines. Onsets come from a small search over the columns of each staff and bar (each column starts where an earlier event ends; costs for voices that stop early, columns no event explains, and columns past the bar's end), with the other staves of the system as a reference. Bar arithmetic (ACC-5) reads beam groups of 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10 or 12 as tuplets with no printed number when the bar does not add up, and `unfilled_bars` lists the voices that do not fill their bar.
  - **Check:** `scripts/check_notes.py` reads each PDF and scores it with the harness's own comparison (`metrics.compare_scores`), so no MusicXML is needed yet. It reports accuracy by job, exact and engraver-input pairs, the error mix, onset and tuplet errors, and the unfilled bars scored as a flag file. `--min-accuracy` makes it fail below a floor.
  - **Development set:** exact pairs (MuseScore 4) 98.1 percent, engraver-input pairs 95.9 percent; MuseScore 3 98.2 and 98.1, MuseScore 4 98.1 and 98.1, LilyPond 94.1, Verovio 93.1.
