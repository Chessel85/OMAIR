I am a blind musician and want to develop an algorithm and application that can very accurately convert an image / pdf of sheet music to music XML and music Braille.
There are already solutions available for this but the accuracy isn't good enough for me.
Can Claude help devise strategies for such an algorithm and implement?
I want to make use of AI where feasible and useful to improve accuracy.
For now, it is assumed that input pdf files into the OMR algorithm will be outputs from engraving software tools like Finale, Sibelius and MuseScore rather than handwritten engravings from out of copyright sources.  
Only if good success is achieved with these will a further phase looking at older engravings be started.
However, an important format ultimately is where a photo or scan from a music book has been taken, and the algorithm must extract scores from amongst noise like blocks of text, graphics and features like the surface the book is resting on or the centre line of the pages. 
To facilitate testing, a corpus of pdf files and accurate music XML equivalents is needed and can be assumed to be available for the purposes of design and development.
Ifd needed, some pdf's can be printed out and photos/scans of them provided.
Accuracy means getting the structure right and a high percentage of note pitch, position and duration.  Performance info like crescendos is highly desirable but not as critical.  Cosmetics like beams is not essential as the music XML output will likely be fed into engines like MuseScore which will handle rendering.
