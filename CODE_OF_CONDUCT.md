# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be welcoming and useful to everyone, including blind and partially sighted musicians and developers, and people of every background, ability and level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Describe images and screenshots in text.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, contributions or participants that break this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainer at ckgoodwin85@gmail.com. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
